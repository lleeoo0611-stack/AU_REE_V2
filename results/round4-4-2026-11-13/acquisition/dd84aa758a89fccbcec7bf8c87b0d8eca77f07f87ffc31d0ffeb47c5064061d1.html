      Mining Tenements (DMIRS-003)

      Live and Pending Mining Tenement Data referencing both surveyed and unsurveyed mining tenements as they appear spatially within the Department of Energy, Mines, Industry Regulation and Safety TENGRAPH system.

      This dataset was formally known as Mining Tenements (DMP-003)
