# Primary-evidence and national hierarchy expansion

See [scope, sources and interpretation](../../docs/phase2-primary-expansion.md).
Cutoff: **2026-11-03**. Execution ID and exact source/code/config/output hashes are in provenance.json.

- `entity_hierarchy.csv`: source-qualified relations, retaining PROJECT, PROJECT_CANDIDATE, DEPOSIT_SCOPE and SITE distinctions.
- `resources.csv`: five issuer-scoped resource observations, with original basis, date and transformation lineage.
- `gis_value_trace.csv`: twenty final metrics (road/operational rail/high-voltage power/port × five scopes), each bound to coordinate and layer SHA.
- `project_domain_evidence.csv`: all six domains, with descriptive/primary evidence and scorable flags kept distinct.
- `issuer_resource_infrastructure/`: five-scope, five-criterion ranking and weight/rounding/method/function/deletion experiments.
- `issuer_resource_composition/`: two comparable complete magnet-oxide shares, separate from NdPr-only values.
- `official_wa_resource_screen/`: twenty government summary rows, exploratory and not issuer-harmonized.

Do not compare ranks across these different panels as though they share one scale.
All rank frequencies refer to the specified preference experiment, not mine success probabilities.
Complete six-domain and Development cohorts remain empty because comparable recovery and financing
evidence is absent. Host-type contrasts are descriptive and retain the representative GA deposit scope.
