Home Geoscience Australia > Scientific topics > Minerals > Critical minerals Critical minerals

Australia’s critical minerals quietly power many of the technologies that modern life increasingly depends on. They also underpin our national security and renewable energy transition.

The Australian Government’s Future Made in Australia agenda supports the net zero transition while strengthening Australia’s economic and national security. It focuses on expanding sovereign capability, manufacturing and adding value to Australia’s resources, including critical minerals. It also aims to attract and enable investment, while investing in the people and communities that will drive national success.

Geoscience Australia’s Resourcing Australia’s Prosperity initiative is a 35-year Australian Government investment in precompetitive geoscience. This initiative focuses on mapping Australia’s potential for critical minerals and strategic materials, hydrogen and carbon storage, and offshore renewable energy. It plays a key role in unlocking new opportunities that support a thriving, resilient national economy and a Future Made in Australia.

What are critical minerals?

Critical minerals are important for everyday technology, clean energy and Australia’s security, but their supply can be unreliable.
