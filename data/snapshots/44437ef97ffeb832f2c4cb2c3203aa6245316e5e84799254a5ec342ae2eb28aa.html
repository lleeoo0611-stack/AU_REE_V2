ASX Announcements by Symbol

/api/headline/bhp

parameters:
    * year 
       - 2018 = show Headlines only for Year 2018
       - >=2018 = show headlines from and including 2018 
       - >2018  = show headlines released after 2018
       - <=2018 = show headlines released up to and including 2018
       - <2018 = show headlines released before 2018
    * headlinesPerPage                        
    * pageNumber 
    * sensitive : default value false= show all
    * category:
        - 0 = All
        - 1 = Annual Reports & Half Year Reports
        - 2 = Quarterly Reports
        - 3 = AGM
        - 4 = Presentations
        - 5 = Others
        - 6 = Annual Reports
        - 7 = Half Year Reports
        - 8 = Financial Reports
        - 9 = Dividends
        - 10 = Issued capital
        - 11 = Stock Exchange Announcement  
        - 12 = All without Presentations and financial reports  
        - 13 = NTA
        - 14 = corporate governance
        - 15 = All announcements (no presentations)
        - 16 = Annual, Half Year & Quarterly Reports
